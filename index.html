About Nini ⭐

Nini is a 3rd grader who loves math and likes seeing how much she can improve with practice. Learn With Nini started as a family project built around her daily math routine — a 10, 15, or 20-minute challenge, focused skill games, and a simple way for other kids to practice alongside her.

💗 Builds ConfidenceSee progress and feel proud of improvement.
⭐ Makes Math FunGames, challenges, and real-life practice.
📈 Builds SkillsPractice one topic at a time and keep growing.
🧠 Growth MindsetMistakes are part of learning.

About Nini ♡

Nini is a 3rd grader who loves math and likes seeing how much she can improve with practice. We started LearnWithNini.com as a family project so she could do a daily 20-minute challenge, turn the skills she was practicing into games, and let classmates and other kids practice alongside her. The goal is simple: make math feel fun, challenging, and worth coming back to every day.

Let’s make math an adventure!

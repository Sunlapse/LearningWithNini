Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Learn With Nini is a family-created math website for kids and families. We try to collect as little information as possible while still making the games and leaderboards work.

Information used by the site

You do not need to create an account or provide an email address to play the math games. If you choose to join a leaderboard, the site uses a nickname, an emoji avatar, game scores, level, best streak, and a randomly generated device identifier used to support leaderboard functions. A nickname and some preferences are also stored in your browser using local storage.

Leaderboard data

Leaderboard information is sent to our database provider, Supabase, so scores can appear on the public leaderboard. The leaderboard is intended for nicknames only. Do not enter a child's real name, email address, phone number, school name, home address, or other identifying information. You can also choose an anonymous Math Star name.

Technical information

Our website hosting and service providers may receive standard technical information needed to operate and secure the site, such as IP address, browser or device information, and request logs.

Third-party links

Learn With Nini links to YouTube for math videos. When you leave LearnWithNini.com, the privacy practices of that service apply.

How we use information

Information is used to operate the games, save basic preferences, display leaderboard scores, improve the site, and protect it from misuse. We do not sell personal information.

Children and families

This site is intended to be used with family or teacher awareness. Children should use only a non-identifying nickname on the leaderboard and should not enter real names, email addresses, phone numbers, school names, home addresses, or other identifying details.

Questions or deletion requests

For privacy questions or requests to remove leaderboard information, contact ninioh888@gmail.com.

We may update this policy as Learn With Nini adds or changes features.
